Re-opening Cookie Preferences (Footer & Widget Methods)

Once a visitor accepts or rejects cookies, the banner closes. LearnPress Cookie Consent provides 3 flexible methods to allow visitors to reopen their preferences at any time.

Method 1

Standard Footer Navigation Link

HTML / Menu Link

Place a text link in your theme footer or Privacy Policy page. Clicking it reopens the modal instantly.

© 2026 LearnPress Academy. All rights reserved.
Privacy Policy Terms of Service
<a href="#" onclick="LP_Cookie.openSettings(); return false;" class="cookie-settings-link">Cookie Settings</a>
Method 2

Shortcode & Gutenberg Block Button

[learnpress_cookie_settings]

Insert a styled button into any page, sidebar widget, or course description using a shortcode or block.

Manage Your Privacy Rights

Click the button to update your consent selections anytime.

[learnpress_cookie_settings text="Cookie Preferences" class="btn-primary"]
Method 3

Floating Trigger Badge (Fixed Bottom Left)

Floating Badge

A discrete floating cookie icon badge fixed at the screen corner (see bottom-left of your screen now!).
